How it works

Five steps. Four of them are ours.

Most customers are surprised how little they have to do. Fill a box and leave it as it is — staples, paperclips, folders and all.

01

You box it. We collect.

Use your own boxes or ours — flat-packed with lids, €39.50 + VAT per pack of ten, delivered anywhere in Ireland. Every box is barcoded at your premises, so it is tracked from the moment it leaves your hands.

photo — boxes being barcoded at collection

02

We prep. Leave the staples.

All documents are prepped on site by Cyclone before scanning. No need to remove any staples. We supply a guide and template for recording the file descriptions used for indexing.

photo — prep bench, staples being removed

03

We scan and index.

Any document from A3 to A6. One box or a forty-year backfile — no order is too big or too small. Every file is indexed to the template you filled in, so it lands searchable rather than as a pile of images.

photo — production scanner detail

04

You get the files.

Images are returned in the digital format that suits you — PDF/A, searchable PDF, TIFF or JPEG — through a secure file transfer portal.

photo — searchable PDF on screen

05

Then you decide about the paper.

Nothing is destroyed without your authorisation.

Quick guide

Frequently asked questions

What type of documents can you scan?

We can scan any type of documents from size A3–A6.

I only have one box. Can you scan small orders?

Yes, no order is too big or too small. If you are paying staff to scan, we can also arrange to bulk scan daily, weekly, monthly, or even quarterly.

Do I need to prepare my documents?

All documents are prepped on site by Cyclone before scanning, so there is no need to remove staples. We supply a guide and template for recording the file descriptions used for indexing.

What happens to my documents after scanning?

Three options: returned to you, stored at our facility until you are happy they are no longer required, or securely shredded at our premises by Cyclone Shredding Limited.

How can you ensure complete confidentiality?

Documents are managed by our barcode-based tracking system, which provides a comprehensive audit trail for every box and file movement, and stored in security-controlled warehouses protected by intruder alarms, smoke detectors and CCTV.
