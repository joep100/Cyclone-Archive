Retention

How long you have to keep it

Every record has a point before which destroying it is a problem, and a point after which keeping it is one. Where those two points fall depends on the document and on who regulates you.

We do not set retention policy. You or your advisers decide how long each class of record is held, and we hold it accordingly — then destroy it, with a certificate, once you authorise that the period has ended.

Record typea contract, a personnel file and a purchase invoice are not the same
Your regulatorprofessional bodies and sector regulators set their own expectations
Contract termsclients and insurers often require longer than the statutory floor
Your own advicethe final call sits with you, not with your storage provider

Destroyed too early

An inspection, an audit or a dispute arrives and the record that would have answered it is gone. Nothing here is shredded without your written authorisation, precisely because that call cannot be undone.

Kept indefinitely

Storage cost compounds, and personal data is not supposed to be held for longer than it is needed. Knowing what you hold is the first step to deciding what you no longer have to.

Quick guide

Shredding questions

How do I know when documents can be destroyed?

That depends on the record and on who regulates your sector, so the retention period is yours to set — with your own advisers if needed. We hold the boxes until you tell us a period has ended, and destroy nothing without your authorisation.

Do I get proof that documents were destroyed?

Yes. Shredding is carried out on our premises by Cyclone Shredding Limited and a certificate of destruction is issued.
